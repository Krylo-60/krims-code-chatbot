Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026 • Krylo's Skybase Sub Verifier

This Privacy Policy applies to the Krylo's Skybase Sub Verifier application ("the App", "we", "us", or "our"), operated by Krishiv (71krishivpb@gmail.com). This policy explains how we collect, use, process, and protect your information when you access or use our automated subscriber verification services for the Krylo's Skybase Discord server.

1. Information We Collect and Receive

When you use Krylo's Skybase Sub Verifier to link your YouTube subscription with your Discord account, we access only the minimal necessary data required to deliver the verification service:

  • Google Account & YouTube Subscription Data: With your explicit consent via Google OAuth 2.0, we request read-only access to your YouTube subscription list using scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly.
  • Discord Account Identifiers: When initiated from Discord, our system receives your public Discord User ID and Guild ID to know which account and server to grant the verified role to.

2. Purpose of Data Processing

Google API Scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly

The sole purpose of requesting access to your YouTube subscriptions is to verify whether your account is currently subscribed to channel UCDPcL5F_EB2MiWN1nJZbDbQ (Krylo on YouTube).

  • Role Assignment: If the subscription is confirmed, our Discord bot automatically assigns the 🔴 Krylo Subscriber role to your Discord profile.
  • No Account Alterations: We do not upload videos, write comments, modify subscriptions, edit playlists, or change any account settings.

3. Data Storage, Retention, and Deletion

We do not retain or store your Google user data.

  • Ephemeral In-Memory Verification: Temporary OAuth access tokens and API responses are processed entirely in server volatile memory during the active verification check and are purged immediately after execution.
  • No Database of Personal Details: We do not store your name, email address, password, private videos, viewing habits, or profile metadata in any database.
  • Data Deletion Requests: Because no personal Google user data is stored on our servers, there is no retained data to delete. However, you may request manual verification audits or role removals at any time by contacting us at 71krishivpb@gmail.com.

4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Disclosures

We do not sell, rent, lease, trade, or share your Google user data with any third parties, marketing firms, or data brokers.

No user data obtained through Google APIs is used to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

5. Google API Limited Use Requirements

Krylo's Skybase Sub Verifier's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. How Users Can Revoke Access

You have full control over your Google Account permissions at all times. You can disconnect Krylo's Skybase Sub Verifier and revoke access immediately through Google's official security portal:
