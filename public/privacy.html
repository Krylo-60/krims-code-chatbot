Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Krylo\'s Skybase Sub Verifier provides YouTube subscription verification for members of Krylo\'s Skybase Discord server.

Information We Collect

When you verify via Google Sign-In, we request read-only access (youtube.readonly) solely to check if your account is subscribed to @krylomcyt.

How We Use Information

We only inspect your active subscription status to assign the corresponding Discord role. We do NOT store your personal Google passwords, or browsing history.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data.
