Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026 • Krylo's Skybase & Krims Code AI

This Privacy Policy describes how Krylo's Skybase and Krims Code AI ("we", "us", or "our") collect, handle, and protect your information when you interact with our Discord bot, web application, and YouTube subscription verification services.

1. Google OAuth & YouTube Data Access

Our application offers an automated verification system allowing Discord community members to verify their subscription to the YouTube channel Krylo (@krylomcyt).

Requested Google Scope: youtube.readonly

We request read-only access to your YouTube subscription list (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly) solely for the purpose of checking whether you are subscribed to channel UCDPcL5F_EB2MiWN1nJZbDbQ (@krylomcyt).

  • No Account Changes: We never modify, edit, delete, or create YouTube content, comments, or playlists.
  • No Personal Profile Storage: We do not collect, retain, or store your Google account password, emails, browsing history, or private videos.
  • Ephemeral Processing: OAuth access tokens are held in temporary memory solely during the active verification request and are cleared immediately upon completion.

2. Discord Data We Process

When executing automated subscriber verification via our Discord bot, we record:

  • Discord User ID: Used to assign the 🔴 Krylo Subscriber role in Krylo's Skybase Discord server.
  • Verification Status: A boolean indicator verifying that your channel subscription check was successful.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, rent, or transfer your personal data to external third parties. We do not use user data for advertising, machine learning model training on personal information, or external marketing campaigns.
