Google OAuth & Data Privacy Transparency

To maintain absolute transparency with our users and comply with Google API Services User Data Policy, here is exactly how Google Account data is accessed:

Requested Scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly
  • Purpose of Request: Read-only access is used strictly to verify that the authenticating user is currently subscribed to Krylo's YouTube channel (UCDPcL5F_EB2MiWN1nJZbDbQ / @krylomcyt).
  • Zero Personal Data Retention: We do NOT store your Google profile information, email inbox, passwords, or browsing history. The subscription check is executed ephemerally in real-time.
  • No Modification Rights: The requested scope is strictly read-only. Our application cannot edit, upload, comment, delete, or modify any aspect of your YouTube account.
  • Immediate Token Discard: OAuth access tokens are discarded from memory immediately after your subscription status is verified and reported back to Discord.
  • Revoke Access Anytime: You can disconnect and revoke permissions instantly through your Google Account Permissions Dashboard.
